How to read these ratings

Repeated TfL arrival predictions produce sampled on-time or late estimates. These are not confirmed train movements. Missing predictions are not cancellations.

Each event updates the original bounded rating model, with restoring pressure toward 1,500. Ratings stay between 100 and 3,500. Service status comes separately from TfL; the archive never contributes to live counts.
