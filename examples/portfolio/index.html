where these ratings come from

The collector records TfL's actual line-status feed every 10 minutes and keeps 30 days of observations. Elo starts at 1500. Good service supports higher ratings; disruptions and cancellation reports count against them. Cubic band gravity pulls ratings toward 1500, increasingly strongly near the edges. Hard limits keep every rating between 100 and 3500. The rating uses the project's Python scoring model, with service-closed periods excluded. It measures observed service quality, not official punctuality or your journey time.

Opening this page requests current TfL service information and recalculates the same model with that observation. Repeated refreshes replace the current ten-minute observation rather than adding votes. The collected history keeps growing independently of your browser. GitHub's scheduled jobs can run late; the timestamps above show the data you actually have.
